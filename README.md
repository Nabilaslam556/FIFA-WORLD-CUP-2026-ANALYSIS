\# ⚽ FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Performance Analysis



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project presents a comprehensive \*\*Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)\*\* of a FIFA World Cup Player Performance dataset. The objective is to understand player, team, and match performance by exploring statistical patterns, identifying trends, detecting outliers, and discovering relationships among different performance metrics.



The analysis transforms raw football statistics into meaningful insights that can help coaches, analysts, scouts, and football enthusiasts evaluate player contributions and overall tournament performance.



\---



\## 🎯 Objectives



\- Understand the structure and quality of the dataset.

\- Analyze player demographics and physical characteristics.

\- Evaluate offensive and defensive player performance.

\- Compare team and nationality performance.

\- Identify top-performing players using multiple performance metrics.

\- Study relationships between player rating and match statistics.

\- Detect data patterns, trends, and outliers through visualization.
